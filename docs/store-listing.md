## Long description outline

1. **Hook:** One tap starts your fast. See progress, time left and what is happening in your body.
2. **Protocols:** 12:12, 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, 5:2, 36 h and custom schedules (Pro).
3. **Stages:** a plain-language guide from the fed state to a deep fast, with honest evidence notes.
4. **Reminders:** fast start, halfway, one hour left, fast complete and eating-window closing, with quiet hours.
5. **Streaks and stats:** daily streaks, weekly freeze, charts and weekly insight (Pro).
6. **Weight and water:** simple logs, charts and goals.
7. **Privacy:** no account, no ads, your data stays on your device.
8. **Subscription terms:** Fastline Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (annual with 3-day free trial, monthly, weekly) or a one-time lifetime unlock. Payment is charged to your store account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in App Store or Google Play account settings. Any unused trial portion is forfeited on purchase.
9. **Disclaimer:** Fastline provides general information only and is not a medical device or medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional before fasting if you have a medical condition, take medication, are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or have a history of disordered eating.
