## 1. Who we are

Fastline ("the app", "we") is an intermittent fasting timer and tracker published by J. P. Dash. For privacy questions, contact jpdash.creator@gmail.com.

## 2. Data stored on your device

Fastline stores the following only in the app's local storage on your device:

- Fasts: start and end times, chosen protocol, and any mood and notes you enter.
- Weight entries (with optional notes) and water entries.
- Settings and preferences: goal, experience level, meal times, units, theme, language, reminder choices, custom protocols, and your acknowledgement of the safety notice.
- A cached record of your Pro purchase status, so Pro keeps working offline.

This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server operated by us. If you use device backups (such as iCloud or Google backup), your operating system may include the app's data in those backups under your own backup settings, and that is governed by Apple or Google.

## 3. Data we do not collect

- No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number is required or collected by the app.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or tracking software. The app contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not send usage events anywhere.
- No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
- The app makes no network requests of its own to a developer server. The only network activity is performed by the Apple or Google store software on your device to process purchases.

We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not receive any.

## 4. Purchases and payments

Fastline Pro is sold as subscriptions or a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play, using the store's in-app purchase system (through the open-source react-native-iap library, which talks directly to the store on your device).

- Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We receive no card, billing or bank details.
- The app reads your purchase status from the store on your device to unlock Pro. It does not send that information to us.
- Apple and Google may share limited purchase information with the developer (for example sales and subscription reports) under their own policies. That information is governed by the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Notifications and permissions

- Reminders (such as fast start, halfway, one hour left, and eating-window closing) are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not operate a push notification server and do not send remote notifications.
- On Android the app requests the notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS). On iOS it asks for permission to send notifications. You can decline or change this at any time in your device settings; the timer still works without it.
- The in-app purchase library adds the store billing permission on Android so that purchases can work.
- The app requests no other sensitive permissions.

## 6. Exporting and sharing your data

Exports are always started by you. From Settings you can export fasts, weight or water history as a CSV file, which opens your device's share sheet. You can also create a share-card image of your progress and share it. Once you send a file or image to another app or person, it is handled by them under their own policies. We do not receive it.

## 7. Deleting your data

- Settings > Delete all data removes your fasts, weight and water entries, notes and settings from the app and cancels scheduled reminders. The app returns to its first-run onboarding. The cached Pro purchase status is kept so Pro can still be restored, and your purchases remain with the store.
- Uninstalling the app removes all data the app stored on your device. Backups you made through iCloud or Google are controlled by you in your device settings.
- Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting data or uninstalling. Cancel them in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

## 8. Children

Fastline is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Fasting is not suitable for children and teenagers. If you are under 18, please do not use the app.

## 9. Health information

Fastline is a tracking and educational tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Fasting stage information is general and educational. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before you start fasting, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or take medication. The health-related entries you make stay on your device as described above.

## 10. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, India DPDP Act)

We do not collect, hold or process your personal data on any server we control, so we do not act as a controller or processor of your fasting or health entries. In practice:

- Access, portability and correction: your data is already in your hands on your device. You can view and edit it in the app and export it as CSV.
- Erasure and withdrawal of consent: use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app. There is nothing for us to erase on our side.
- Objection, restriction and automated decisions: we do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.
- California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information, and do not collect it through the app.
- India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): we do not collect or process your digital personal data. You can exercise your rights over locally stored data directly in the app.
- Purchase and account data held by Apple or Google is controlled by them. Please contact them to exercise your rights over that data.

If you have a question about your rights, contact jpdash.creator@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 11. International users

Because your data stays on your own device and is not transferred to us, we do not transfer your personal data across borders. Apple and Google process purchase data under their own policies and may do so in other countries.

## 12. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security features (for example your lock screen and device encryption). The app does not transmit your entries over the network. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device updated and protected.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app adds a feature that handles data differently. We will change the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version at the same address. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the app or in the store release notes before it takes effect.
